⚔️ Mass Save Roller — Roll20 Link

  1. Open your Roll20 game and the website in this Chrome.
  2. On the website tick Roll the d20s in Roll20 and press the button.
  3. Roll in Roll20, e.g. /r 6d20. The website fills in the saves.

After updating this add-on, press reload on chrome://extensions and refresh both tabs.
