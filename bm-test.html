🔖 Bookmark test (10 seconds)

  1. Press Ctrl + Shift + B if you can't see your bookmarks bar.
  2. Drag this gold button up onto your bookmarks bar:
    ⚔️ Mass Save
  3. Go to your Roll20 game tab and click the new ⚔️ Mass Save bookmark.

If a gold box saying “Mass Save bookmark works in Roll20!” pops up at the top of Roll20, it works. If nothing happens, it's blocked.

This test changes nothing in your game.
