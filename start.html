⚔️ Mass Save Roller

Roll saving throws for a whole pile of monsters at once. You type the DC, the modifiers and the damage — it does the rest.

Open the website

Works on its own — no add-on needed if you roll on the website.

Using it with Roll20 — nothing to install

Drag this gold button up onto your bookmarks bar (press Ctrl+Shift+B if you don't see the bar):

⚔️ Mass Save

Then, in your Roll20 game, click the ⚔️ Mass Save bookmark. The roller opens in its own window, linked to Roll20.

Click it once each time you open or reload Roll20. Changing maps is fine.

How a mass save goes

  1. In Roll20, select the monster tokens caught in the spell.
  2. In the roller click ⬇ Get selected tokens. Type their save modifiers (once), the DC and the damage.
  3. Click Start, then in Roll20 type /r 8d20 (one d20 per monster).
  4. Done — passes and fails are worked out and the selected tokens' HP goes down in Roll20. Tokens you didn't select are never touched.

Tokens you made from your own images work too — if their HP bar is empty, the roller fills it in with “HP each”.
