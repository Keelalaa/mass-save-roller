How to link Roll20 (get the bookmark)
  1. Drag this button onto your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B shows the bar): ⚔️ Mass Save
  2. In your Roll20 game, click the bookmark. This window opens linked to Roll20.
  3. Click it again whenever you reload Roll20.
