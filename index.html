How to install the Roll20 add-on (one time)
  1. Download the add-on and unzip it somewhere you'll keep it.
  2. In Chrome go to chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped roll20-addon folder.
  4. Refresh this page and your Roll20 tab.
